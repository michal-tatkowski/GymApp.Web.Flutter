GymApp is a gym workout log that doesn't just record — it tells you what to do next time.

TRAIN SET BY SET
• Workout plans from ready-made templates (full body, upper/lower, push/pull/legs, 5×5) or your own
• Progression hints: when to add weight and when to add reps — RIR included
• Supersets, circuits and drop sets, a rest timer with a notification
• A warm-up and stretching fitted to the workout, a plate calculator
• A timer for timed sets (plank, hang), swap a busy machine for an exercise of the same muscle group
• Exercise notes ("seat on 4") and how the workout felt

PROGRESS YOU CAN SEE
• Records and charts for every exercise (volume, estimated 1RM, heaviest weight)
• A year-long activity map, this week and month compared with the previous ones
• Sets per muscle group, with a hint about what is missing
• Goals with a forecast ("at this pace: about 6 weeks"), monthly challenges and badges
• Body weight, measurements and progress photos

MADE FOR THE GYM FLOOR
• Works offline — workouts are sent when you're back online
• Large one-handed buttons, dark theme and pure black (OLED)
• A "This week" home-screen widget and workout reminders
• kg or lb, English and Polish

YOUR DATA
• Export everything (JSON and CSV) and delete your account in the app
• No ads, no selling data
